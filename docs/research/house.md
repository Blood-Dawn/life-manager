# House Module Research

1. **Auto-reschedule next due date from completion date, not the calendar (low effort)** — Chore Master's mechanic (next due date calculated from when you actually finished, not a fixed slot) stops one missed day from cascading into permanently-overdue recurring chores. https://tidywell-app.com/blog/top-adhd-chore-apps-2026

2. **Dueness indicator instead of a binary overdue flag (low-medium effort)** — Tody replaces fixed schedules with a "dueness" percentage shown as a green-to-red gradient rather than a shaming red badge. https://todyapp.com/method, https://todyapp.com/faq

3. **Zone tag + zone filter (low effort)** — FlyLady's 5-zone rotation and its app port HomeRoutines cut overwhelm by narrowing focus to one area of the house per week instead of the whole house at once. https://organizingmoms.com/flylady-zones/, http://www.homeroutines.com/flylady/

4. **Built-in short work-timer on a chore ("Start a 20/10") (low effort)** — UFYH's 20-minutes-on/10-minutes-off structure is the specific mechanic people credit for actually starting instead of avoiding a task. https://www.unfuckyourhabitat.com/whats-a-2010/

5. **Forgiving streaks / no punitive overdue shame (low effort)** — A 2026 comparison of ADHD chore apps found apps with red overdue badges and brittle streaks lose ADHD users within three weeks, while apps that soften the shame retain them. https://tidywell-app.com/blog/top-adhd-chore-apps-2026

6. **One-tap snooze instead of open-edit-save (low effort)** — Directly addresses the friction that causes list pileup.

7. **Assignee field shared across the household (low effort)** — OurHome's core loved feature is assigning a chore to a specific family member with a shared view. **Not applicable here** — Life Manager is explicitly single-user (every signed-in user only ever sees their own data), so a cross-household assignee field doesn't fit the app's scope and was excluded for that reason, not for effort.

## Folded into the spec
Items 2 and 5 combined into a simple 3-color due-soon/overdue scale (a scaled-down "dueness" without building Tody's full percentage engine), item 6 (one-tap snooze), and item 3 (zone field + filter chips). Items 1, 4 left out for one-day scope; item 7 excluded as out of scope for a single-user app. Sweepy's leaderboard/gamification and Tody's full dirt-percentage engine were skipped as the "clone trap" the roadmap warns against.
